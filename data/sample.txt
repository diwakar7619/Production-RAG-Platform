Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming the way humans interact with technology. Machine learning enables computers to learn from data without being explicitly programmed.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines vector search with large language models. Instead of relying only on the model's internal knowledge, RAG retrieves relevant documents from a vector database before generating a response.

Vector databases such as Qdrant store embeddings, which are numerical representations of text. During retrieval, user queries are converted into embeddings and compared with stored vectors using cosine similarity.

This approach reduces hallucinations and improves the accuracy of AI systems by grounding responses in retrieved documents.
